1. Introduction 
1.1 Purpose 
The Doctor App is designed to enhance the workflow of medical professionals by providing 
tools for appointment management, professional networking, research sharing, and peer 
collaboration. This app will help doctors manage their practice efficiently while fostering a 
professional medical community. 
1.2 Scope 
The Doctor App will allow: 
• Appointment booking and management with patients. 
• Social media-like interactions for professional engagement. 
• Research sharing among medical professionals. 
• Direct communication with other doctors. 
• Verification of doctors before granting access to the platform. 
• Doctors to add clinics in their profile and create custom timing for each weekday. 
• Doctors to follow each other and interact with posts through likes, comments, and 
saves. 
• Payment gateway and QR code options for doctors to receive payments. 
• Payment management section where doctors can track received payments and 
request settlements. 
• Doctors to track the number of visitors who have seen their research. 
• Doctors to save and comment on each other's research documents.

• Doctors to manage medical records of their patients by uploading prescriptions, 
test reports, and case history. 
• Doctors to reschedule appointments after completion. 
• Azure cloud server and storage for secure and scalable hosting. 
1.3 Intended Users 
• Verified Medical Doctors 
• Junior Doctor Students (limited access) 
• Admins (for doctor verification and content moderation) 
1.4 Tech Stack 
• Frontend: Flutter (for Android App) 
• Backend: Node.js with Express.js

platform, enhancing trust and security. Doctors must sign up and submit verification 
documents, which are reviewed by the admin team before approval. Once verified, they 
can update their profiles, set consultation fees, and define their availability. This ensures 
that patients and fellow doctors interact with credible professionals, maintaining the 
platform's integrity and quality of service.

2.4 Appointment Management 
Doctors can efficiently manage patient appointment requests through an integrated 
scheduling system. They can set their available time slots for each clinic, accept or reject 
appointment requests, and reschedule them if necessary. The system will display an 
overview of all appointments for the day, ensuring doctors stay informed about their 
schedules. 
After an appointment is completed, doctors will have the ability to upload prescriptions 
and necessary medical notes directly to the platform. Patients will receive notifications 
and can access these records at any time. Additionally, doctors can reschedule follow-up 
appointments within the system, making continuity of care more efficient. 
Doctors will also have a dedicated dashboard to view pending appointment requests, 
upcoming confirmed appointments, and history of past consultations. Automatic 
reminders will be sent to both patients and doctors before each scheduled appointment to 
minimize no-shows.
